# Al-Ma’thurat reading order — Aku Islam source (v2.3.0)

Reference: https://akuislam.com/panduan/al-mathurat-sughra-kubra-rumi/ (updated 4 October 2026; reviewed 1 November 2026).

## Sequence

- Kubra Pagi and Petang: all numbered steps 1–49.
- Sughra Pagi and Petang: steps 1–9, 23–40, 44, 45, 47, exactly in that order (30 readings). These are the entries marked 🔰 in the guide.
- Morning/Evening alternatives: steps 26, 27, 28, 29 use morning or evening wording, and the evening Du‘a Rabitah (step 49) has an additional evening opening.
- The old booklet/PDF path is kept as an optional alternative, not the primary Al-Ma’thurat experience.

## Source and integrity

The Aku Islam article is used as an **ordering reference**, not as a text, translation or audio licensing grant. Its audio, images and translations are not copied into this app. Qur'anic Arabic and remaining Arabic phrases in mathurat.js were previously sourced independently. Some du‘a texts, repetition numbers, transliterations, and hadith citations have not received qualified scholarly or editorial verification. Please compare them with an authoritative edition before treating this app as a certified religious reference.

## Acceptance criteria

The four original collection IDs open native reading mode with 30/30/49/49 entries. All IDs resolve to Arabic text; the Sughra members match the 🔰 markers; evening variants are selected where appropriate; legacy booklet viewing remains optional. Offline caching must include mathurat.js.
